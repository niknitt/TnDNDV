This project focuses on analyzing and optimizing the arrangement of inter-sheaths within single-core high-voltage (HV) cables. Inter-sheaths are thin metallic layers placed between the core and the outer sheath of the cable. Their role is to control the voltage distribution across the insulation. By optimizing the number, position, and voltage levels of these inter-sheaths, we can reduce the electrical stress acting on the insulation layer. This helps in enhancing cable reliability, preventing breakdowns, and improving the overall lifespan and safety of the high-voltage cable system
